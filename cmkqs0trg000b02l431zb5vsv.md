## Phase 0: The Strategy (So you don’t waste months)

### What to post: Value-first stories (the 4:1 rule)

If your feed is 80% “buy now”… you’re invisible.

Run **4:1**:

* **4 posts:** teach / show / break down / behind-the-scenes
    
* **1 post:** offer / CTA / “try the product”
    

Creators love this too. Because it keeps their audience warm.

### How to collaborate: The Pyramid (Big for reach, small for sales)

Two creator tiers. Two jobs.

**Big Creators (100K+):** reach. launches. social proof.  
**Micro Creators (10K–50K):** trust. clicks. signups.

Layer them.

![A pyramid chart showing influencer tiers, illustrating Instagram strategy for SaaS sales growth with big and micro creators.](https://cdn.marblism.com/3q1Et0ccO-4.webp align="left")

### How to scale: Find lookalikes

One winner is nice.

A repeatable pipeline is the whole game.

### Step-by-step: find any niche in minutes

**Step 1 — Start with a niche keyword** Pick the *audience*, not the product.

Examples:

* AI: “ai tools”, “chatgpt”, “ai productivity”
    
* B2B: “startup marketing”, “cold email”, “revops”

**Step 2 — Add filters that matter** Use filters like:

* platform (Instagram / YouTube / TikTok / X)
    
* location (if you sell geo-specific)

* engagement thresholds (avoid dead accounts)
    

**Step 3 — Flip the search: scan a competitor brand** Want the fastest shortcut?

Search for creators who already talk to your buyers by starting from a **competitor brand** and seeing the creator ecosystem around them.

## Phase 2: THE “VIEW DETAILS” LAB (where deals are won)

Discovery gives you a list.

**View Details** tells you who’s real… and who’s a trap.

Don’t get hypnotized by follower count.  
Open the profile. Go deeper.

### 2.1 Engagement Breakdown (is the audience real?)

What you’re checking:

* is engagement consistent or spiky?
    
* are comments human or copy-paste?
    
* does engagement match follower size?
    

You’re looking for *signal*, not perfection.

![Napkin-style minimalist bar chart titled Engagement Breakdown showing real vs suspicious engagement.](https://cdn.marblism.com/Cbt4oHb7fhz.webp align="left")

Quick founder checklist:

* **good:** steady likes + real comments + stable posting
    
* **bad:** huge followers + low comments + weird spikes
    

If it smells fake, it usually is.

### 2.2 Content Performance (what actually goes viral?)

This section is your content R&D lab.

Look for:

* top posts by views/engagement
    
* formats that win (talking head, POV, carousel, screen-record)
    
* hooks that repeat
    
* topics that spike
    

This isn’t about copying a post.

It’s about extracting patterns:

* “they always start with a painful truth”
    
* “they use 3-step frameworks”
    
* “they show the result in the first 2 seconds”
    

Then you brief creators with *what works*.

### 2.3 Past Collaborations (the killer feature)

This is the unfair advantage.

In **Past Collaborations**, you can see:

* which brands this creator worked with
    
* how often they do paid collabs
    
* what competitors are already buying attention here
    

Use it for:

* **conflict checks** (avoid creators already married to a rival)
    
* **pricing leverage** (you’ll know they take deals)
    
* **competitor strategy theft** (the smart kind)
    

Founder-to-founder: this is how you reverse-engineer your category.

If 3 competitors keep paying creators in one micro-niche…  
that niche is printing money.

## Phase 4: THE BRAIN (Ask AI)

At this point you have:

* niche
    
* proof
    
* patterns
    
* a scalable list
    

Now move fast.

Use **AI Insights (Ask AI)** for two jobs:

### Job 1: understand what content works

Ask things like:

* “What hooks are common in the top posts?”
    
* “Which topics drive saves vs comments?”
    
* “What’s the best format for this creator’s audience?”
    

You get a clean summary you can plug into briefs.

### Job 2: generate scripts instantly

Ask:

* “Write 5 Reel scripts in this creator’s style for \[pain\] + \[product category\].”
    
* “Turn this winning post pattern into a 30-sec script.”
    
* “Make a founder-led version and a creator-led version.”
    

Then send creators *tight* briefs:

* hook options
    
* storyline beats
    
* CTA that doesn’t feel like an ad
    

Remember the 4:1 rule. Keep it value-first.

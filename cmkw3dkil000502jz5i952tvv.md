## The Platform Breakdown (Quick Facts)

Let's get practical about what each platform actually offers.

### YouTube Shorts

* **60-second limit**
    
* Better for **evergreen content** (tutorials, tips, educational stuff)
    
* Stronger **long-term discovery** via search and recommendations
    
* **Monetization** through ad revenue sharing
    
* Thumbs up AND thumbs down feedback
    
* Massive music library with fewer copyright headaches
    

### Instagram Reels

* **90-second limit** (more storytelling room)
    
* Better for **trend-driven content** (fashion, lifestyle, aesthetics)
    
* Stronger **shareability** through DMs and Stories
    
* More **creative tools** (filters, stickers, polls, templates)
    
* Can create Reels from photos using templates
    
* Integrates with your existing Instagram ecosystem
    

**Reach potential?** Nearly identical. Instagram Reels reaches 2.4 billion users. YouTube Shorts hits 2.3 billion.

The difference isn't reach. It's **how** your content gets discovered and **how long** it stays relevant.

## The Decision Framework

Still unsure? Here's a quick filter.

**Go heavier on YouTube Shorts if:**

* You already have a YouTube channel
    
* Your content is educational or tutorial-based
    
* You want passive discovery over time
    
* Monetization through ads matters to you
    
* You prefer detailed audience feedback
    

**Go heavier on Instagram Reels if:**

* You're already active on Instagram
    
* Your content is trend-driven or aesthetic-focused
    
* Shareability and DM virality matter
    
* You want more creative editing tools
    
* You're building a personal brand alongside a product
    

**Or... do both.** Just let the data tell you where to double down each quarter.

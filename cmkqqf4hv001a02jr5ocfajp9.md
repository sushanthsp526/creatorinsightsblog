## Step 4: The 4:1 Ratio (The Only Way This Works)

If you sell on every post, people leave. Simple.

Your audience needs proof you’re worth following *before* you ask for anything.

So use the **4:1 ratio**:

**Give value 4 times. Sell 1 time.**

Here’s a clean way to think about it:

| Post Type | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Value #1 | Customer result / case study breakdown |
| Value #2 | Behind-the-scenes build story |
| Value #3 | Trend or teardown in your niche |
| Value #4 | Founder lesson (a mistake you won’t repeat) |
| Sales | One clear offer + one clear CTA |

Why it works: **trust compounds**.  
By the time you post a “buy” post, it doesn’t feel spammy. It feels fair.

## Step 5: Find Your Growth Partners

Here's something most SaaS founders ignore: you don't have to grow alone.

Collaborations accelerate everything. The right partner introduces you to an audience that already trusts them.

But how do you find the right collaborators?

Inside the Creator Insights dashboard, check out the **Similar Creators** and **Collaboration Insights** tabs.

You'll see:

* Accounts with overlapping audiences
    
* Creators who've done successful collabs in your space
    
* Potential partners you probably haven't discovered yet
    

This isn't random outreach. It's strategic. You're finding people who already talk to your ideal customers.

Reach out. Propose a joint Reel. A carousel collab. A Story takeover. These partnerships compound over time.

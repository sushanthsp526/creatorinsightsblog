## Train AI on Your Voice (Stop Sounding Like ChatGPT)

Here's the problem with most AI content: it sounds like AI content.

The solution? **Voice training**.

Feed your AI tool (ChatGPT, Claude, or Creator Insights' Script Generator) 3-5 examples of your best-performing captions or scripts. Tell it:

* "This is how I write hooks"
    
* "This is my brand tone"
    
* "I never use these words"
    

![AI learning system connecting to content scripts showing voice training for creators](https://cdn.marblism.com/Uy4Igm5d87-.webp align="left")

Then prompt it like this:

*"Write a 60-second YouTube script in my voice about \[topic\]. Use the tone and structure from the examples I gave you. Keep it under 150 words."*

Suddenly, your AI-generated drafts don't need 20 minutes of editing. They need 2 minutes.

**Creator Insights Script Generator** is built for this. Upload your top 5 scripts, and it learns your rhythm, humor, and pacing. Every new script comes out sounding like you: not like a robot trying to sell enterprise SaaS.

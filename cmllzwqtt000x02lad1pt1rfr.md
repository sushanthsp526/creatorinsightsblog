## The Spreadsheet Graveyard Problem

Let's be honest about what happens with spreadsheets.

You copy-paste an Instagram handle. Then you manually grab their follower count. You screenshot their engagement rate from some third-party tool. You add their email (if you can find it). You mark them as "contacted" in another column.

Two days later, that follower count is wrong. The creator deleted their contact info. You can't find the DM thread. Your "contacted" column means nothing because you forgot to update it after sending that second follow-up.

![Chaotic spreadsheet workspace with errors showing failed manual creator management system](https://cdn.marblism.com/0MaogjN5VOR.webp align="left")

Manual data entry is the enemy of scale. It's slow, it's error-prone, and it doesn't give you the real-time insights you need to make fast decisions.

Plus, here's the killer: When you're managing 100+ creators across multiple campaigns, you need more than just data. You need a system that knows who you talked to last Tuesday, what they said, and when you should follow up.

A spreadsheet can't do that. It's just a dead document sitting on your hard drive.

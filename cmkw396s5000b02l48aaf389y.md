## Step 3: Decode Their Top Performing Content

Okay. You've identified your competitors. You've got their metrics.

Now let's dig into the good stuff: their actual content.

Inside Creator Insights, you can see a full **Top Post History** for any handle. This shows you:

* **Top organic posts** : What's getting shared and saved naturally
    
* **Top performing ads** : What they're paying to promote (and why)
    
* **Historical performance** : Patterns over time, not just recent wins
    

This is gold.

Because here's what most people don't realize: the posts a creator promotes as ads are usually their highest-converting content. They've already tested it organically. They know it works.

So when you see what they're running as ads? You're seeing their A-game.

![Side-by-side panels showing organic and paid post performance, visualizing top content comparison and analytics](https://cdn.marblism.com/_LBACiDC9Zz.webp align="left")

### Organic vs. Ads: The Hidden Signal

Pay attention to the gap between organic hits and paid promotions.

Sometimes a post performs great organically but they never run it as an ad. That's a missed opportunity for them… and a signal for you.

Other times, they're pushing ads on content that didn't perform organically. That tells you they're testing something new.

Either way, you're getting insider intel.
